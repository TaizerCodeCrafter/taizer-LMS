Build a modern, responsive educational website for an Economics teacher in Sri Lanka. The website should serve as a teacher portfolio, student acquisition funnel, and future LMS platform.

## 🎯 Main Goal

Attract students (Grade 6–13), build trust with parents, and convert visitors into paid class enrollments via WhatsApp and future LMS.

---

## 🏠 Pages Required

### 1. Home Page (Landing Page)

Sections:

* Hero Section with teacher image, title: “Economics Classes Grade 6–13”

* Tagline: “සරලව Economics ඉගෙන ගමු”

* CTA buttons: “Join Now” and “Free Class” (link to WhatsApp)

* About Teacher (short intro with qualifications and experience)

* Courses Overview (Grade 6–9, O/L, A/L with cards)

* Results / Achievements (student success)

* Sample Lesson (embedded YouTube video)

* Testimonials (students/parents)

* Final CTA (Join WhatsApp Class)

---

### 2. About Page

* Full teacher biography
* Academic qualifications (Economics degree)
* Teaching experience
* Mission and teaching philosophy
* Professional images

---

### 3. Courses Page

Display structured course categories:

* Grade 6–9 (foundation)
* O/L Economics (exam-focused)
* A/L Economics (theory + past papers)

Each course should include:

* Description
* Price
* Schedule
* “Enroll Now” button

---

### 4. Course Details Page (Dynamic)

* Course title
* Full syllabus breakdown
* Lesson format (online/live via Zoom)
* Duration and schedule
* Pricing
* CTA: Enroll / Join WhatsApp

---

### 5. Free Resources Page

* Free notes (PDF downloads)
* Short educational videos
* Exam tips
* Designed for SEO and lead generation

---

### 6. Results / Testimonials Page

* Student reviews
* Exam results (A/L, O/L)
* Parent feedback

---

### 7. Contact Page

* Phone number
* WhatsApp button
* Email
* Contact form

---

### 8. Login / Student Portal (Future LMS)

* User authentication (login/signup)
* Student dashboard
* Course access (locked for now, scalable later)
